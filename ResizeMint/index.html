A shorter path to the right size

Skip the editor when resizing is all you need.

ResizeMint is made for Mac users who regularly prepare images for websites, documents, messages, app listings, and design handoffs. Select an image in Finder, right-click, and choose a percentage or exact pixel preset. The resized copy is created without interrupting your workflow with a full editor.

When the job needs more control, drag in one image, many images, or a folder. ResizeMint gives you a clear preview, linked width and height controls, output information, format-aware quality settings, and flexible export locations.

Common questions

About ResizeMint

What makes ResizeMint different from an online image resizer?

ResizeMint is a native Mac utility that works directly with Finder and processes images locally. You do not need to upload files to a website or repeat the same dimensions every time.

Can it resize several images at once?

Yes. Drag multiple images or a folder into ResizeMint, adjust the queue, preview the output, and export the current image or all images together.

Can I create my own Finder presets?

Yes. Percentage and exact pixel-size presets are configurable in ResizeMint and then appear in its Finder right-click menu.

Where can I get ResizeMint?

ResizeMint is distributed for macOS exclusively through the Mac App Store.
